Do not include PHI

Before any content goes into a portfolio file, note, or AI prompt:

  • Never include patient identifiers — Names, initials, MRNs, dates of birth, room/bed numbers.
  • Watch for identifying combinations — A rare procedure + a date + a unit can point at one specific patient.
  • Use roles instead of names — “my preceptor,” “charge nurse” — never “Jane R. on 4 West.”
  • No screenshots or photos of work — EHR screenshots and printed MARs violate privacy outright.
  • No secrets, ever — Logins, badge numbers, API keys stay out of files and prompts.

Full rules: PHI Safety.

The three rules of this manual

  1. Never put PHI in your portfolio, notes, or AI prompts. See PHI Safety.
  2. Your portfolio describes you, not a patient. If it reads oddly out loud in a hallway, rewrite it.
  3. AI drafts, you verify. Certificates, check-off sheets, and your state board are the sources of truth.
